How an import moves

Five movements, one steady hand.

From the moment the manifest lands to the moment the tailgate closes, each stage has an owner, a clock and a paper trail.

  1. 1

    T−72 h · Pre-arrival

    Manifest & pre-clearance

    Documents received three days before berthing. HS codes checked, duties estimated, declarations drafted while the vessel is still at sea.

  2. 2

    Day 0 · Berth

    Berth & discharge

    The vessel takes her berth on the tide. Cranes work the bay plan; your containers are tallied straight into a reserved yard block.

  3. 3

    Day 0–1 · Customs

    Clearance & inspection

    Declaration lodged the same day. If customs selects the box, our officers meet the inspection at the quay — no queue, no silence.

  4. 4

    Day 1–2 · CFS

    Devan, sort & stage

    LCL cargo is devanned, checked against manifest, re-labelled and staged by route. Damage photographed and reported within the hour.

  5. 5

    Day 2–3 · Delivery

    Gate-out & final mile

    Trucks take booked slots at Gate 4. You watch the run live; the consignee signs, the POD lands in your inbox, the file closes.
